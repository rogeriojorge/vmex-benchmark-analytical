# Status

Working branch `research/exact-equilibrium-verification`, from main `f904b3a`. One record of where the study stands; update it with each pushed milestone.

## Goal

A reproducible verification of equilibrium fields and their sensitivities against exact solutions (Landreman integer and sheared families, Issan asymmetric extensions, Solov'ev, López vacuum D), across VMEX, VMEC2000, VMEC++ and DESC, with matched physical norms, resolved quadrature, certified derivatives and at least one established numerical mechanism. Verification only: no claims about stability, confinement or experiments, and no claim to be the first exact-solution verification (Landreman 2026, Panici et al. 2023, Hudson et al. 2025 precede it).

(1) Axisymmetric Solov'ev (both parities): every protocol and both codes converge to fsq 1e-14 in 0.2-2.4 k iterations; B error 2.4e-5 (NS 17) to 8e-6 (NS 129) is 180-10^4 times the projection floor and does not fall with NS (J 1e-3, force 1.5e-2): the plateau is consistent with the spectral-condensation force, since the same exact seed with `tcon0 = 0` falls roughly as NS^-1.5 to NS^-2 to 1.9e-6 at NS 129 (symmetric case only; the asymmetric case does not improve). (2) Integer 3-D (tau 0, +-0.5): no run reaches 1e-14; every cold, seeded and laddered run exhausts 20000 iterations per stage with fsq 1e-9..1e-12 and a drifting axis; best B 2e-4 (tcon0 seed, NS 129) and otherwise 6e-4..9e-4 at NS 33-129 (floor 1e-7..6e-7); the exact seed leaves the exact state within the iteration cap and lands where the cold run does; the NS ladder is no better than cold (integer 3-D: B 1e-2 with up to 0.7 % of the volume not evaluable; VMEC2000 tau: 1.2e-2).
