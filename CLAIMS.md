# Claim ledger

Every claim of the study, the status label it has earned, and the index records (`results/index.json`) that carry the evidence. Verification only: nothing here is a statement about stability, confinement or experiments, and the study is not the first exact-solution verification (Landreman 2026, Panici et al. 2023, Hudson et al. 2025 precede it). Statements about a code hold for the pinned identities in [ENVIRONMENT.md](ENVIRONMENT.md).

## Labels used

| Label | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| analytically verified reference | closed-form field, quadrature and derivatives agree with an independent evaluation to roundoff-level tolerance; no solver involved |
| sampled chart check | a coordinate chart or inversion tested on a finite sample, not proved |
| certified chart | interval or algebraic certificate over the whole chart domain (earned only for the upstream cubic-root domain of Lopez D, see R2) |
| finite-basis projection | the exact state truncated to the VMEC/DESC basis, scored against the exact field; no solve |
| optimizer termination | how a solve ended (iteration cap, tolerance, limit cycle); not a statement about accuracy |
| nonlinear residual qualification | the force residual of the returned state is at the stated tolerance |
| physical error qualification | error against the exact field on exact-chart points with resolved quadrature and a recorded quadrature uncertainty |
| discrete derivative check | a derivative or operator quantity certified against the discrete operator itself (root, tangent, transpose, finite differences, dense Jacobian) |
| continuum sensitivity check | the discrete derivative compared with the exact derivative, with a convergence ladder |

Mechanism claims are attributed by intervention (a quantity is switched off or replaced and the effect measured); the label column names the measurement behind them. B, J and force errors are relative L2 over the whole volume on exact-chart points, J = curl B / mu0, force against the exact grad p.

## Open problems

1. Near-axis first-shell discretization layer: only the bias part is removed. Untested: refining the first cells (the radial mesh is uniform in s), an alternative axis closure in VMEX.
2. Whole-volume sensitivity gates fail for the same reason; the continuum sensitivity check is earned for the bulk only.
3. 3-D sensitivity: pointwise response off by O(1); the frozen m = 1 pair drift is not the whole explanation.
4. Integer 3-D tau = +-0.5: the NS 33-129 plateau has no established mechanism; the polar seed does not improve the NS 17 solved state.
5. 3-D drift: why the discrete energy has a weakly unstable lambda m = 1 direction at the exact integer state; whether VMEC2000 shares it (its hot start is unusable in the PARVMEC build).
6. Lopez D: why fsq limit-cycles at 1e-8 to 1e-4 from a valid seed; the VMEC Hessian null space; the cold-start basin.
7. Issan family B: no code converged; VMEX stalls at 1e-7 from the exact geometry (an unrotated, pre-X3 run) and DESC stays at 1e-2 to 1e-3. Not re-examined after X3.
8. Work-precision: VMEX/VMEC2000 time ratio on loaded points, GPU, VMEC++ in the matrix.
9. Pinned STELLOPT 3e1439d is unavailable; VMEC2000 results come from office build 70c637a and macOS build ee17550, and stalled asymmetric states differ between them.
10. Mechanism WOUTs (73 states) are not archived; they regenerate from the command in `results/mechanism/wout_manifest.json`.
