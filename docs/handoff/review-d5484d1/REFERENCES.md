# Sources and how to use them

Reviewed on 2026-09-24. Repository observations are pinned below. An open pull request is an experimental implementation, not an accepted result or a released capability. The review read source and public records through GitHub, and papers/documentation through their primary websites. It did not rerun the repository's solver experiments.

## Primary scientific and numerical references

**[L1] M. Landreman, _Analytic toroidal 3D MHD equilibria and steady Euler flows with invariant surfaces_ (2026).** [arXiv:2609.26742v1, HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.26742v1). The integer-family oscillator identity in equations (9)-(14) supplies exact field-line evolution as well as equilibrium references. The sheared family supplies nontrivial flux inversion and current-prescribed transform checks. Do not confuse the trajectory parameter with physical cylindrical angle or charged-particle time. [Supplement pinned at 4c0b690](https://github.com/landreman/analytic_3d_equilibria/tree/4c0b690ddebdc71811c88223eb9f44a98ab64222).

**[L2] SciPy, `quad` / QUADPACK interface.** [Official documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.integrate.quad.html). Explicit breakpoints handle known locations of difficult integrands. Here they must be native spline knots in the integration coordinate, or their computed crossings in reference coordinates. Adaptive quadrature is not a reason to disguise an unconverged physical domain or coordinate map.

**[L3] SciPy, `CubicSpline`; C. de Boor, _A Practical Guide to Splines_.** [Official CubicSpline documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.interpolate.CubicSpline.html). A cubic spline is C2; derivatives above that require one-sided or piecewise interpretation at knots. The magnetic field in VMEX's native Clebsch reconstruction already contains first geometry derivatives, so geometry smoothness cannot be assigned unchanged to B.

**[L4] R. Becker and R. Rannacher, _An optimal control approach to a posteriori error estimation in finite element methods_, Acta Numerica 10 (2001), 1-102.** [DOI 10.1017/S0962492901000010](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0962492901000010). Dual-weighted residuals motivate goal-oriented refinement. For VMEX, first test an enriched-grid defect/adjoint estimate against actual refined objective changes. The finite-element theory is not automatically a rigorous certificate for the present mixed spectral/finite-difference residual and gauge constraints.

**[L5] D. Panici, R. Conlin, D. W. Dudt and E. Kolemen, _The DESC Stellarator Code Suite Part I: Quick and accurate equilibria computations_.** [arXiv:2203.17173](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.17173). High-order representation and axis regularity provide an independent native comparison. Use common physical norms, boundary error budgets, and independent radial/angular refinement; do not quote a historical paper as a ranking of the current states.

**[L6] R. Conlin, D. W. Dudt, D. Panici and E. Kolemen, _The DESC Stellarator Code Suite Part II: Perturbation and continuation methods_.** [arXiv:2203.15927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15927). Supports predictor/corrector continuation along boundary, pressure and transform directions. A perturbative prediction is an initial guess until the corrected root and physical observables are checked.

**[L7] Z. Tecchiolli et al., _Constructing nested coordinates inside strongly shaped toroids using an action principle_.** [arXiv:2405.08173](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.08173). A bounded coordinate-initialization comparison for difficult sheared shapes. Minimize coordinate distortion while keeping physical surfaces fixed; this is not an alternative proof of force balance.

**[L8] GVEC, _The Galerkin Variational Equilibrium Code_.** [Official documentation](https://gvec.readthedocs.io/latest/) and [theory](https://gvec.readthedocs.io/latest/user/theory.html). Arbitrary-degree radial B-splines and flexible mappings give a useful representation-control experiment. Freeze the actual version/source and inspect normalization before using WOUT import or a third-party adapter.

**[L9] T. M. Antonsen Jr., E. J. Paul and M. Landreman, _Adjoint approach to calculating shape gradients for 3D magnetic confinement equilibria_.** [arXiv:1812.06154](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.06154). Physical shape-gradient and reciprocity tests complement discrete JVP/VJP checks. Keep boundary and profile constraints identical when comparing formulations.

**[L10] D. Malhotra, A. J. Cerfon, M. O'Neil and E. Toler, _Efficient high-order singular quadrature schemes in magnetic fusion_.** [arXiv:1909.07417](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.07417). Source separation and exterior-field evaluation need independent singular and near-singular quadrature studies. This supports the operator stage before coupled free-boundary optimization.

**[L11] E. Toler, A. J. Cerfon and D. Malhotra, _A fast, accurate, and easy to implement Kapur-Rokhlin quadrature scheme for singular integrals in axisymmetric geometries_.** [arXiv:2211.00493](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.00493). A compact independent axisymmetric virtual-casing comparator, with logarithmic-singularity assumptions that should not be applied unchanged to a general 3-D integral.

**[L12] JAX root and linear-solve differentiation.** [`custom_root`](https://docs.jax.dev/en/latest/_autosummary/jax.lax.custom_root.html), [`custom_linear_solve`](https://docs.jax.dev/en/latest/_autosummary/jax.lax.custom_linear_solve.html), and [`custom_vjp`](https://docs.jax.dev/en/latest/_autosummary/jax.custom_vjp.html). Read the contracts at the installed version: mathematical root and linear-solve invariants are obligations of the caller. A reverse-only wrapper does not establish forward-mode support. Use the actual residual tangent operator when a public forward transformation is unsupported.

**[L13] J. Schilling, _The Numerics of VMEC++_.** [arXiv:2502.04374](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04374). Use the derivation and implementation conventions to compare force blocks, profiles, half/full meshes, normalization and constraints. Verify current features in source rather than inheriting a preprint's earlier feature list.

## Scope and attribution

The six probes in this handoff are independent mathematical/implementation-contract illustrations. Their outputs are not new VMEX, DESC, GVEC, free-boundary, or kinetic measurements. The exact-flow formula is a use of Landreman's oscillator construction, not a claimed new equilibrium family. The diagonal-stretch extension is retained from the preceding plan and must keep its derivation and domain restrictions.
